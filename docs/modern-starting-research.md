No subsequent investment returns, outcome narrative, or later corporate events were researched for selection. Search results sometimes contain unrelated retrospective hits; these were excluded. Original articles, corporate releases, and official macro releases are distinguished from their issue indexes and from contemporary secondary reports of other organizations' data.

The headline pools are editorial discovery pools, not exhaustive or randomly sampled news corpora. Pool topics are short original paraphrases, not original headlines. Links in the source-key tables are direct public archive references, and every candidate row resolves to one of them. Do not imply all fifty were the period's fifty most important stories.

## Gaps and assembly cautions

- TIME is overrepresented in the discovery pools. Selected feeds add original BLS, central-bank and corporate announcements plus CNN/Fortune reporting. This is a public archive compromise, not a balanced corpus claim.
- Original issue dates are safe conservative cutoff dates for index-only candidates; dates on distinct selected TIME article pages sometimes precede their issue by eleven days (September 11 versus September 22). Retain both when helpful and never substitute a modern crawl date.
- CPI and employment use original archived releases. The May employment release and April CPI HTML/text variants failed while their PDFs worked. No need to substitute revised modern series.
- Confidence is a documented contemporary secondary approximation in both dates; original Conference Board releases were not inspected. All other macro readings cite original public agency reports. Consumer-confidence surveys do not incorporate events after their sample collection.
- May H.15 is one week older than a theoretical month-end yield. Its weekly 10-year value 4.74% was checked against the original row and header. A May31 public release was not verified; no later publication was relabeled as available in May.
- SPF probabilities must state the exact quarter and mean/median convention. The May probability is unverified in this note; growth forecast is a sound default. August's professional survey is stale by September30 and should be visibly dated.
- There is no claim of an exhaustive candidate search, no unsupported data-quality exclusion, and no claim that professional growth projections predict any asset's return.
