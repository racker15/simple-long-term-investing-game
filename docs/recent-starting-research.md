## Professional outlook

The original SPF PDF explicitly prints **Release Date: February 12, 2016** on page 1. It surveyed **40 forecasters**. Forecasts were received by February 9; the questionnaire was sent January 29. Annual growth was expected to remain positive, while the 2016 annual-average growth forecast was lower than three months earlier. The same release projects annualized growth of 2.0% for 2016 Q1 and 2.5% for Q2. These quarterly rates must not be confused with the annual-average 2.1% forecast. No recession probability is claimed here. This is contemporaneous primary evidence, not a present-day SPF summary or a later forecast revision.

Suggested player text: “Professional forecasters expect continued growth in 2016, but their growth outlook has weakened.”

## Data conventions and unresolved items

Yahoo publicly describes historical **Close** as adjusted for splits and **Adjusted Close** as adjusted for splits and dividend and/or capital-gain distributions on its history pages. Its official help gives the split and dividend adjustment convention: https://in.help.yahoo.com/kb/adjusted-close-sln28256.html . Generic current-page search metadata was consulted only for column definitions, not to investigate the selected future window. A public chart API is not presented here as a documented supported API: no official API contract was located. Do not substitute a timestamp at the start of a month for the last tradable observation of the preceding or same calendar month. The reliable proposed transformation is to fetch only the permitted daily window, select the final observed trading day of each calendar month, preserve the actual observation date and original payload hash, and calculate consecutive adjusted-close ratios. Reject null, nonpositive, missing-month or duplicate observations. Current Yahoo adjustments are retrospective and need explicit limitations; source-level uniform adjustment scaling cancels in price ratios, but future corporate actions still require separate qualification.

No Yahoo price window was fetched in this research. No stock future window was opened. Starting stock trailing performance and public-source payload feasibility remain unverified, and are exact outstanding evidence required before qualification. Broad-asset trailing calculations can reuse the existing normalized series, but were not calculated in this note. Archive HTML source pinning must be performed by the main builder; browsing inspection alone is not a reproducibility hash. The TIME issue pool overweights one magazine and includes January stories; disclose this editorial limitation rather than claim exhaustive February news reconstruction.
