# Early historical starting evidence

This note contains only evidence available by August 31, 1982 and January 31, 1987. Selected stocks were proposed from contemporary attention before any later price series or outcome was examined. Proposed 1982 offerings are Apple, IBM, and Coca-Cola; proposed 1987 offerings are IBM, General Electric, and Apple. Price feasibility and historical security identities are separate checks, not outcome-based editorial selection.

The JSON records provide claim-level source IDs and direct primary-source URLs. Federal releases preserve their original release/embargo dates. TIME dates are original issue dates shown by its publisher archive; modern archive or digitization dates are unknown and are not treated as first-public dates. COMPUTE! copies are contemporary magazine transcriptions with an original monthly issue label; the exact original publication day is unknown, and the conservative month-end date is explicitly an approximation.

There are 50 actual, dated TIME table of contents candidates per cutoff and 8 selected stories per cutoff. Candidate labels are paraphrases of index topics; only selected articles and stock-salience articles received body-level verification. The selections cover business/economy, international politics, science, sports, culture or domestic news; no automatic relationship between a headline and an offered stock is implied. These discovery pools are a reproducible archive sample, not an exhaustive census of all news then available.

Forecasts are public Federal Reserve institutional projections, explicitly labeled ranges. They are not SPF observations, medians, or realized outcomes. [July 1982 report](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/FRB/1980s/frb_081982.pdf) gives 1983 real GNP growth 2.5–4%, and [July 1986 report/public testimony](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/bog_mpo/bog_19860718.pdf) gives 1987 central tendency 3–3.5%. The latter is an older public forecast vintage; no later January forecast was verified.

The six macro entries expose their evidence limits. 1982 now has a contemporaneous [August 24 AP report](https://newspapers.swco.ttu.edu/bitstreams/e2c56eda-a80a-486c-84bb-9d40e513e045/download) giving July twelve-month CPI-U inflation 6.5%, and an [August 5 Christian Science Monitor report](https://www.csmonitor.com/layout/set/amphtml/1982/0805/080551.html) giving July consumer confidence 61.3 on the original 1969–1970 base. Original BLS/Conference Board releases were not retrieved; contemporary reporting establishes public availability. The September 1982 CPI Detailed Report is corroboration only. Its experimental rental-equivalence CPI-U-X1 rate 6.4% must not replace the original CPI-U 6.5%. 1987 CPI 1.1% uses contemporary January 21 Associated Press reporting; the original BLS release was not retrieved. Its later February 1987 Detailed Report is corroboration only. 1987 consumer confidence remains unverified; a clearly labeled bank prime borrowing rate supplies an alternative sixth card. If the UI requires confidence specifically, 1987 remains visibly incomplete rather than receiving a fabricated value.

For original 1987 AT&T and GM, current tickers alone do not establish continuous issuer history. Modern T history can represent SBC rather than original AT&T; modern GM starts with its 2010 IPO. They remain contemporary candidates but require proper historical series and are not selected for offering here. Other legacy candidate ticker mappings also require verification before using prices.
